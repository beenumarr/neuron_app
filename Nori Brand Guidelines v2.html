Clinical trust.

Nori is an AI dietitian that reads your health context, understands the food in front of you, and answers in plain language, always saying what it measured against. The brand has to feel like a credible health product built for Africa, not a cooking app and not surveillance.

Trusted Intelligent Warm Never scolding

Brand idea

"Intelligence that grows from food." Three ideas live in one mark: a leaf (nutrition, farming, nori itself), a pulse line (clinical care, health data) and a single amber node (the AI that notices). Nature on the outside, science and intelligence inside.

Logo

The symbol is a leaf carrying a heartbeat, with an amber AI node at its tip. Always use the supplied vector shapes; never retype or redraw.

nori
Wordmark

Sora ExtraBold, all lowercase, tight tracking (-2%). Lowercase keeps it friendly and approachable. Symbol sits left, height equal to the x-height plus ascender.

Clearspace and size

Keep clear space equal to the amber node diameter on all sides. Minimum 20px for the symbol alone, 72px wide for the full lockup.

Don't

Recolour the leaf, remove the node, add gradients or shadows, rotate the pulse line, or place on busy photos without a solid panel.

Colour

Deep teal carries trust and clinical credibility. Nori green is the living, growing colour of nutrition and agriculture. Amber is reserved for AI moments and gentle attention, never alarm.

Interface language

Rounded, calm, data-forward. Pills for actions, 16-20px radius for cards, 48px minimum touch targets, thin 1px borders instead of heavy shadows. Icons: Lucide, stroke 2.

Voice

Like a knowledgeable dietitian who respects you: specific, kind, unhurried. Always cite what the answer is based on. Never shame, never diagnose.

Say

"That is high in salt against the limit in your profile. Try less stock cube next time."

Not

"Unhealthy choice. Avoid."

Say

"I estimated the portion from your photo. Adjust it and I will recalculate."

Not

"Analysis complete."

Imagery and graphics

Real Nigerian and African meals shot from above in natural light: egusi, jollof, tuwo, akara, fruit, local markets. Real people, never stock-photo clichés. Graphics use soft leaf shapes and the pulse line as a divider. Data visuals use Green for progress, Amber for attention, Coral only when a limit is exceeded.

Where we stop

Nori is a decision-support and education tool, not a medical replacement or diagnostic system.

Shown on first run, in the health profile, and on every exported report. Nori never diagnoses, prescribes or treats, and refers people to a clinician when a question crosses that line.
